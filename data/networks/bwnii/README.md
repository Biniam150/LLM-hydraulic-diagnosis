# BWN-II input

`BWNII_1_Wu.inp` is the study's Wu solution variant of the BWN-II/D-Town benchmark. It is copied without modifying the model; its original location and hash are in `results/publication_manifest.csv`.

The manuscript cites Marchi et al. (2014) for benchmark background. The solver is EPANET with demand-driven analysis. This variant's asset counts can differ from other D-Town distributions. Confirm source attribution and redistribution terms before public release.
